Current status: demo website

OBNOTE currently shows fictional sample products. There are no active advertisements, affiliate purchase links, or checkout services on this site.

1. Affiliate and advertising disclosure

If OBNOTE adds affiliate links in the future, those links will be clearly identified. We may receive a commission from eligible purchases, which does not normally increase the price you pay. We will update this notice and relevant product pages before enabling such links.

We do not currently run advertising banners or paid product placements on this demo website.

2. Product details

Sample products (codes 1001–1004) are fictional and are provided only to demonstrate the product search interface. When real listings are added, prices, specifications, and availability should be checked directly with the seller.

3. Product-code searches

The search field runs in your browser using publicly available product data. The product catalog is supplied through GitHub Pages and Supabase, a third-party database hosting provider. OBNOTE does not currently provide user accounts, checkout, or forms for submitting your personal information.

4. Cookies, analytics, and hosting

Our current website code does not set advertising cookies or operate its own visitor analytics. The public OBNOTE address uses Vercel as a reverse proxy for pages published on GitHub Pages, while the product catalog uses Supabase's hosted API. These service providers may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, according to their privacy practices. See GitHub's privacy statement, Vercel's privacy policy, and Supabase's privacy policy.

Future product photos may be loaded from external image providers, and clicking an external seller link will take you to that provider's website, where its own terms and privacy policy apply.

5. Changes to this page

We will update this page before adding analytics, advertising, purchase links, sign-in features, or other services that change how information is handled.
